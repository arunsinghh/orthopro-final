About OrthoPro Artificial Limbs Center

A certified prosthetics & orthotics rehabilitation center built on one belief: every person deserves to move with dignity.

Our Story

OrthoPro Artificial Limbs Center was founded by clinicians who saw too many amputee families overpaying for poorly fitted devices — or giving up on mobility entirely. We set out to build a center where international technology meets honest, personalized care at prices Indian families can afford.

Today our in-house workshop, 3D scanning suite and gait-training floor serve patients from across Delhi NCR and neighbouring states, from first-time fittings to complex revisions referred from other centers.

  • Certified Prosthetist & Orthotist team
  • 2,000+ successful fittings across all amputation levels
  • In-house fabrication workshop — no outsourcing
  • ADIP scheme, CGHS & insurance paperwork support
  • Home visits for patients who cannot travel
